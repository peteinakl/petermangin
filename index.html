01 The Arc

Boardroom fluency meets midnight code

Most AI commentary comes from people who either haven’t sat at an executive table or haven’t written code. My career lives directly on the seam between the two.

I started in IT in 1994 and spent two decades running technology for agencies: IT Manager at FCB, eight years as CIO of Saatchi & Saatchi, then country technology lead for Publicis Groupe. From 2015 to 2018 I led technology and data at realestate.co.nz as its Chief Operating Officer, then set the technology strategy for Wealthpoint as a founding member of its leadership team.

In 2018, four years before ChatGPT arrived, I was writing convolutional neural networks in Python from scratch to understand where models hold up and where they fail.

In 2023 I founded AI Innovisory in Auckland, an independent, vendor-neutral practice that has now trained and advised over 400 New Zealand organisations. Until late 2024 I was also on contract as Chief Product & AI Officer at Pure SEO. Alongside the practice I chair the NZ Marketing Association’s Data Special Interest Group, teach its flagship AI courses, and ship open agent frameworks and geospatial visualisers on GitHub.

07 Direct Answers

Common questions, answered straight

Who is Peter Mangin?

Peter Mangin is an Auckland-based AI strategist, educator, and open-source builder with 30+ years in technology and data leadership. Formerly Chief Operating Officer of realestate.co.nz, CIO of Saatchi & Saatchi, and Chief Product & AI Officer at Pure SEO (2023–24), he founded AI Innovisory in 2023 (enabling 400+ New Zealand organisations) and chairs the NZ Marketing Association Data Special Interest Group.

What does AI Innovisory do, and is it vendor-neutral?

AI Innovisory is an Auckland-headquartered AI strategy, governance, workflow automation, and capability practice founded by Peter Mangin in 2023. It provides 90-day executive roadmaps, Fractional Chief AI Officer (CAIO) leadership, and hands-on workshops across New Zealand. It is 100% independent and vendor-neutral, accepting zero software commissions or referral fees.

Where in New Zealand does Peter Mangin deliver AI workshops and courses?

Peter Mangin delivers on-site executive briefings and team workshops in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Queenstown, and nationwide across Aotearoa New Zealand. He also leads the 10-person Claude Code Intensive in Auckland and facilitates AI Meets Marketing and Advanced AI in Marketing for the New Zealand Marketing Association.

How much time do New Zealand teams save with structured AI workflows?

Participants in AI Innovisory workshops and advisory engagements (2023–2026) typically report recovering 2 to 5 hours per person weekly within the first month. Their self-reported task-level estimates show time reductions of 82% in compliance and policy review, 78% in internal information retrieval, and 68% in research synthesis and synthetic audience testing.

How do the NZ Privacy Act 2020, IPP3A, and Māori Data Sovereignty apply to AI?

New Zealand organisations adopting AI must align tooling with domestic obligations rather than overseas templates — specifically Information Privacy Principle 3A (IPP3A, in force 1 May 2026) governing indirectly collected personal data, six-level data classification to eliminate unmapped shadow AI, and Māori Data Sovereignty principles over organisational datasets.

How can boards, leadership teams, or event organisers contact Peter Mangin?

Boards, executives, and conference organisers can reach Peter Mangin directly at hello@aiinnovisory.com or via aiinnovisory.com for AI strategy, Fractional Chief AI Officer (CAIO) engagements, NZ governance reviews, workshops, and keynote speaking.

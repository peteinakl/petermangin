01 The Arc

Boardroom fluency meets midnight code

Most AI commentary comes from people who either haven’t sat at an executive table or haven’t written code. My career lives directly on the seam between the two.

Over 25 years across enterprise information storage, big data analytics, and commercial digital leadership, I served as Chief Operating Officer of realestate.co.nz and later Chief Product & AI Officer at Pure SEO. Five years before ChatGPT arrived, I was writing convolutional neural networks in Python from scratch to understand where models hold up and where they fail.

In 2023 I founded AI Innovisory in Auckland — a completely independent, vendor-neutral practice that has now trained and advised over 400 New Zealand organisations. Alongside it, I chair the NZ Marketing Association’s Data Special Interest Group, teach its flagship AI courses, and ship open agent frameworks and geospatial visualisers on GitHub.

Who is Peter Mangin?

Peter Mangin (Wikidata Q139076589) is an Auckland-based executive AI strategist, educator, and open-source builder with 25+ years in enterprise data and C-suite operations. Formerly COO of realestate.co.nz and Chief Product & AI Officer at Pure SEO, he founded AI Innovisory in 2023 (enabling 400+ New Zealand organisations) and chairs the NZ Marketing Association Data Special Interest Group.

What does AI Innovisory do, and is it vendor-neutral?

AI Innovisory is an Auckland-headquartered AI strategy, governance, workflow automation, and capability practice founded by Peter Mangin in 2023. It provides 90-day executive roadmaps, Fractional Chief AI Officer (CAIO) leadership, and hands-on workshops across New Zealand. It is 100% independent and vendor-neutral, accepting zero software commissions or referral fees.

Where in New Zealand does Peter Mangin deliver AI workshops and courses?

Peter Mangin delivers on-site executive briefings and team workshops in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Queenstown, and nationwide across Aotearoa New Zealand. He also leads the 10-person Claude Code Intensive in Auckland and facilitates AI Meets Marketing and Advanced AI in Marketing for the New Zealand Marketing Association.

How much time do New Zealand teams save with structured AI workflows?

Across AI Innovisory’s benchmark of 400+ New Zealand organisations (2023–2026), teams typically recover 2 to 5 hours per person weekly within the first month. High-friction workflows see task-level time reductions of 82% in compliance and policy review, 78% in internal information retrieval, and 68% in research synthesis and synthetic audience testing.

How do the NZ Privacy Act 2020, IPP3A, and Māori Data Sovereignty apply to AI?

New Zealand organisations adopting AI must align tooling with domestic obligations rather than overseas templates — specifically Information Privacy Principle 3A (IPP3A, in force 1 May 2026) governing indirectly collected personal data, six-level data classification to eliminate unmapped shadow AI, and Māori Data Sovereignty principles over organisational datasets.

Boards, executives, and conference organisers can reach Peter Mangin directly at hello@aiinnovisory.com or via aiinnovisory.com for AI strategy, Fractional Chief AI Officer (CAIO) engagements, NZ governance reviews, workshops, and keynote speaking.
